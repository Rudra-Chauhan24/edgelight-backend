  <section class="faq-section" id="faq">
    <div class="container">
      <div class="section-header text-center">
        <div class="section-tag">Common Questions</div>
        <h2 class="section-title">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
      </div>

      <div class="faq-accordion">
        <details class="faq-item" open>
          <summary class="faq-question">
            <span>How does the automatic camera detection work?</span>
            <span class="faq-icon">+</span>
          </summary>
          <div class="faq-answer">
            Edge Light senses when your webcam activates for a call. Whenever a meeting app (like Zoom or Microsoft Teams) or browser (like Google Meet) starts your camera, Edge Light automatically turns on to illuminate your face. As soon as your camera turns off or your call ends, the illumination smoothly fades away.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="faq-item">
          <summary class="faq-question">
            <span>Will the light block my mouse clicks on tabs or buttons?</span>
            <span class="faq-icon">+</span>
          </summary>
          <div class="faq-answer">
            No. Edge Light is designed to allow normal interaction with your other applications while the lighting effect is active. Mouse clicks, window switching, and scrolling pass directly to your browser tabs, windows, and desktop tools underneath. Only clicking inside the floating dock controls interacts with Edge Light.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="faq-item">
          <summary class="faq-question">
            <span>Can I customize the color temperature and thickness?</span>
            <span class="faq-icon">+</span>
          </summary>
          <div class="faq-answer">
            Yes! Hover over the right screen edge to open the floating dock. You can smoothly slide between 3744K warm candle glow to 6500K cool daylight, and adjust ring thickness from 20px to 180px.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="faq-item">
          <summary class="faq-question">
            <span>What are the global keyboard shortcuts?</span>
            <span class="faq-icon">+</span>
          </summary>
          <div class="faq-answer">
            <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>L</kbd> toggles Edge Light on/off from any application or full-screen game. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Q</kbd> safely quits the application.
          </div>
        </details>
